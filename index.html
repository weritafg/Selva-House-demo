The Concept

Tropical brutalism, in Riverside Park

Selva House is an urban residential development within Riverside Park that merges jungle aesthetics with metropolitan proximity. Designed by Alessandro Pupillo with Outtelier, the project embodies "tropical brutalism" — blending European concrete traditions with South Florida's environmental context.

Cast-in-place concrete serves as both structural and aesthetic material, left exposed in the spirit of béton brut. Deep overhangs, shaded terraces, and open-air spaces respond to the climate, while a modest footprint and generous cantilevers let the building rise organically from the landscape, preserving the mature trees already on site.

  • Two single-family residences
  • Exposed cast-in-place concrete
  • Private pool & landscaped grounds
