Riverside Park, Fort Lauderdale

Set within the canopy

Selva House sits among mature oaks and quiet, tree-lined streets in Riverside Park — close to Las Olas Boulevard, the beach, and the New River.

The Setting

Known as the Venice of America

Fort Lauderdale's canals, river, and coastline have long earned it the nickname "Venice of America." Selva House sits inland in Riverside Park, among established tree canopy, while staying close to the city's beaches, boating culture, and downtown dining and shopping along Las Olas Boulevard.

Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport and the wider South Florida coastline are both within easy reach, making Selva House equally suited to full-time living or a seasonal residence.
